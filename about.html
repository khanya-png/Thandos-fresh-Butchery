About Us

Welcome to Thando's Fresh Produce & Butchery. We are proud to serve the Soweto community with fresh fruits,vegetables, and premium quality meat.

Mission:To provide affordable fresh, anf healthy food to every home. To provide our community with the freshest highest-quality fruits,vegetables, and meat at affordable prices.At Thando's Fresh Produce & Butchery,we are committed to support local farmers,ensuring food safety, and giving every customer friendly,reliable service that feels like family

Vision:To be Soweto's #1 trusted Butchery and produce store.To become the most trusted fresh produce and butchery shop in our cummunity-known for quality, honesty, and bringing healthy, farm-fresh food to every household.We aim to grow into a brand that feeds families, supports local growers, and sets the standard for freshness in South Africa.
