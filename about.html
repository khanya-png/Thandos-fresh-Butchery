About Us

Thando's Fresh Butchery was founded with a passion for providing fresh, high-quality meat to our community. We believe in supporting local farmers and delivering only the best cuts to our customers.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, fresh, and hygienic meat products while maintaining excellent customer service.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted butchery in the community, known for quality and freshness.

Our Values

  • Freshness - Daily fresh stock
  • Quality - Best cuts only
  • Honesty - Fair prices
  • Cleanliness - Hygienic environment
