About Us

Location: 1490 Mthembu Street, Soweto, Johannesburg, 1868

Thando's Fresh Produce & Butchery was started in 2020 by Thando Nkosi with one simple goal - to bring fresh, affordable, and quality meat to the families of Soweto. What started as a small stall has grown into a trusted community butchery loved by many.

Our Mission

To provide 100% fresh, halal-certified, and affordable meat products daily, while delivering excellent customer service and supporting local farmers in Gauteng.

Our Vision

To become the leading trusted butchery in Gauteng, known for freshness, quality, and community upliftment - expanding to online delivery across all of Johannesburg by 2028.

Our Values

  • Freshness: We slaughter and cut fresh daily
  • Quality: Only Grade A meat
  • Affordability: Fair prices for our community
  • Integrity: Honest weight and honest price
  • Community: We buy from local farms and create local jobs
